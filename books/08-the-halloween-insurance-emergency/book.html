BOOK 8 · PIP & WAFFLES

Waffles & Pip and the Halloween Insurance Emergency

An illustrated woodland adventure.

Halloween had arrived in the woodland.

Orange leaves covered the paths.

Pumpkins sat outside burrows.

Tiny lanterns glowed beneath the trees.

And Waffles the white rabbit had been told one very important thing.

“Do not touch anything until everyone gets here,” said Pip.

Waffles nodded.

“I understand.”

Pip narrowed her eyes.

“Do you?”

“Yes.”

1 / 33

Waffles reached toward a pumpkin.

Pip slapped his paw away with one wing.

“Waffles.”

“I was checking it.”

“For what?”

“Pumpkin problems.”

Pip sighed.

Then she disappeared behind a tree.

“Wait here.”

Waffles waited.

For almost seven seconds.

2 / 33

Then Pip stepped back onto the path.

Waffles stared.

Pip was wearing a tiny dark suit.

A tiny red tie.

A tiny hat.

And carrying a tiny brown briefcase.

Waffles blinked.

“Pip?”

“Yes?”

“What happened to you?”

“It’s my Halloween costume.”

Waffles walked around her.

“You look like someone who wants me to sign something.”

Pip smiled.

“Exactly.”

3 / 33

She opened her briefcase.

Inside were papers.

Lots of papers.

Waffles took one step backward.

“What are you supposed to be?”

Pip straightened her tie.

“An insurance salesman.”

Waffles stared.

“A what?”

“Someone who protects you when things go wrong.”

Waffles’ ears popped straight up.

“You can do that?”

“In theory.”

Pip looked at Waffles.

“You are going to need the deluxe plan.”

4 / 33

The rest of the woodland crew began arriving.

Mouse came dressed as a pumpkin.

Squirrel wore a cape.

Mole had a sheet over his head.

Two little eye holes had been cut into it.

Waffles tilted his head.

“Mole?”

“Yes?”

“Are you a ghost?”

“I think so.”

“You have dirt on your ghost.”

“I came through a tunnel.”

“Of course you did.”

5 / 33

Hazel the hedgehog arrived wearing a crown.

Tilly the turtle wore a witch hat.

It kept sliding over her eyes.

Buzz the bee had somehow attached eight fake spider legs to himself.

Waffles screamed.

“GIANT SPIDER!”

Buzz hovered in front of him.

“It’s me.”

“Oh.”

Buzz buzzed away.

Waffles whispered to Pip,

“That costume is too effective.”

6 / 33

Pip pulled out a form.

“Would you like Spider Surprise Coverage?”

“No.”

“You just screamed.”

“Fine. Maybe.”

Pip made a check mark.

Soon everyone started down the woodland path to trick-or-treat.

Waffles carried a little pumpkin-shaped bucket.

Pip carried her briefcase.

“Why aren’t you carrying a candy bucket?” asked Waffles.

“I’m working.”

“It’s Halloween.”

“Insurance never sleeps.”

Waffles nodded solemnly.

“That sounds terrible.”

7 / 33

They reached the first pumpkin display.

Three carved pumpkins sat on a wooden bench.

One smiled.

One frowned.

One had enormous crooked teeth.

Waffles leaned toward the toothy pumpkin.

“Suspicious.”

“It’s decoration,” said Pip.

Waffles leaned closer.

“Very suspicious decoration.”

8 / 33

Suddenly—

WOBBLE.

The pumpkin moved.

Waffles jumped backward.

“IT’S ALIVE!”

The pumpkin rolled off the bench.

BUMP.

Down the path it went.

“RUNAWAY PUMPKIN!” shouted Waffles.

Everyone chased it.

Squirrel ran.

Mouse ran.

Pip flew.

Tilly began turning around.

“I’m coming,” she called.

9 / 33

The pumpkin rolled faster.

Waffles leaped.

“AAAAAA—”

SPLAT!

Waffles landed belly-first in a pile of leaves.

The pumpkin rolled safely into a bush.

Pip landed beside him.

Waffles lifted his head.

Leaves covered his ears.

10 / 33

“Is that covered?”

Pip opened her briefcase.

“Did you deliberately jump at the pumpkin?”

Waffles thought about it.

“Yes.”

“Then no.”

“What if I say the pumpkin attacked me?”

“No.”

“It moved first.”

“Still no.”

Waffles frowned.

“Insurance is complicated.”

11 / 33

They continued down the path.

At the next tree, baskets of treats waited for them.

Acorn cookies.

Berry muffins.

Apple slices.

And tiny wrapped candies.

Squirrel gasped.

“CANDY!”

Everyone rushed forward.

Everyone except Pip.

12 / 33

Pip stood beside a little folding table she had somehow brought with her.

On it was a sign:

PIP’S WOODLAND INSURANCE

SMALL BIRD. BIG PROTECTION.

Waffles stared at it.

“When did you make a sign?”

“Yesterday.”

“You planned this?”

“I know you.”

Fair point.

13 / 33

Waffles picked up a candy.

Pip slid a paper toward him.

“Candy Bucket Loss Protection?”

“No.”

Waffles picked up another candy.

“Sticky Paw Coverage?”

“No.”

He reached for a third.

“Stomachache Protection?”

Waffles stopped.

“…How much?”

“One strawberry.”

“Too expensive.”

Waffles ate the candy.

14 / 33

They continued toward Mole’s part of the woods.

The path grew darker.

Paper bats hung from branches.

Fake spiderwebs stretched between trees.

Little lanterns flickered beside the trail.

Then they reached a wooden sign.

HAUNTED TUNNEL

Waffles stopped.

Pip stopped.

Everyone stopped.

15 / 33

Slowly, Pip turned toward Mole.

“Mole.”

“Yes?”

“What is that?”

“My Halloween tunnel.”

Pip closed her eyes.

Waffles’ entire face lit up.

“A HAUNTED TUNNEL?”

“No,” said Pip.

“I didn’t ask anything.”

“You were going to.”

“I was going to compliment Mole’s craftsmanship.”

“No, you weren’t.”

16 / 33

Waffles stared into the tunnel.

It was dark.

Very dark.

A tiny orange light flickered somewhere inside.

Waffles whispered,

“That looks amazing.”

Pip opened her briefcase.

“No.”

“What?”

“No.”

“I haven’t even asked.”

“You want tunnel insurance.”

Waffles smiled.

“You really are good at this.”

17 / 33

Pip pulled out a thick stack of papers.

“For you?”

She dropped it onto the ground.

THUMP.

“Tunnel coverage requires additional paperwork.”

Waffles stared at the stack.

“All that?”

“That’s page one.”

Waffles looked at the tunnel.

Then at the paperwork.

Then back at the tunnel.

“I’ll risk it.”

Pip groaned.

18 / 33

Mole stepped forward.

“It’s safe. I made it just for Halloween.”

Pip looked suspicious.

“You checked it?”

“Yes.”

“No mystery side tunnels?”

Mole hesitated.

“Mole.”

“One.”

Waffles gasped.

Pip pointed at him.

“Do not.”

“I didn’t do anything!”

“You gasped in a tunnel-related manner.”

19 / 33

Everyone entered.

Pip flew beside Waffles.

Mouse carried a tiny lantern.

Squirrel’s cape dragged in the dirt.

Buzz’s fake spider legs kept getting caught on roots.

Tilly was still approaching the entrance.

Inside the tunnel—

SCRATCH.

Waffles froze.

“What was that?”

“Decoration,” said Mole.

SCRATCH. SCRATCH.

20 / 33

Waffles moved closer to Pip.

“I am not scared.”

“You’re holding my tail.”

“I’m protecting you.”

“I can fly.”

“That is beside the point.”

21 / 33

Then—

BOO!

A paper ghost dropped from the ceiling.

Waffles screamed.

He jumped sideways.

He bumped Squirrel.

Squirrel bumped Mouse.

Mouse dropped the lantern.

Buzz flew into Waffles’ ear.

Mole tripped over his sheet.

And Pip landed face-first on her insurance paperwork.

22 / 33

Everyone went silent.

Pip slowly stood up.

Her hat was crooked.

Her tie was sideways.

One piece of paper was stuck to her forehead.

Waffles whispered,

“Is this covered?”

Pip stared at him.

“No.”

23 / 33

At the end of the tunnel, they reached a tiny underground room filled with glowing pumpkins.

Everyone cheered.

“Happy Halloween!” said Mole.

There were treats everywhere.

Carrot cookies.

Berry candies.

Apple chips.

And one enormous bowl of strawberries.

Waffles’ eyes widened.

“Pip.”

“No.”

“I haven’t asked.”

“You’re thinking loudly again.”

24 / 33

Waffles reached for a strawberry.

Then he stopped.

“What if the strawberry rolls away?”

“It won’t.”

“What if it does?”

“It won’t.”

“What if I chase it?”

“Then you definitely aren’t covered.”

25 / 33

Waffles carefully picked up the strawberry.

He held it with both paws.

“Safety first.”

Pip stared.

“I have never heard you say that before.”

Waffles took a bite.

“I’m growing.”

“You said that before.”

“Emotionally.”

26 / 33

Finally everyone headed back outside.

Tilly was just entering.

“Did I miss anything?”

Everyone looked at each other.

Pip looked at her crooked hat.

Mole looked at his torn ghost sheet.

Mouse looked at the dirt on her pumpkin costume.

Buzz had lost three spider legs.

Waffles had leaves stuck to his tail.

“No,” said Pip.

“Nothing.”

Tilly nodded.

“Good.”

She slowly continued into the tunnel.

27 / 33

Outside, moonlight filled the woodland.

Everyone dumped their candy onto a big blanket.

Waffles sat beside Pip.

Pip finally removed her tiny insurance hat.

“Did you sell any policies?” asked Waffles.

Pip looked at her clipboard.

“One.”

“To who?”

“Hazel.”

Waffles looked across the blanket.

Hazel was carefully wrapping her candy in a leaf.

“What did she insure?”

“Her snack collection.”

Waffles nodded.

“Smart.”

28 / 33

Then he reached into his bucket.

He pulled out the biggest strawberry he had collected all night.

He handed it to Pip.

“For you.”

Pip smiled.

“What’s this?”

“Emergency insurance payment.”

“That is not how insurance works.”

“It should be.”

Pip laughed.

29 / 33

Then—

BONK!

An acorn fell from the tree and bounced off Waffles’ head.

Everyone froze.

Waffles slowly looked at Pip.

Pip slowly picked up her clipboard.

Waffles rubbed his head.

“Please tell me I bought acorn coverage.”

Pip checked the paper.

She smiled.

“You did.”

Waffles threw both paws into the air.

“INSURANCE WORKS!”

Everyone laughed.

30 / 33

Then Waffles noticed something behind Pip.

A small dark hole beneath a tree root.

His ears rose.

Pip didn’t even turn around.

“No.”

Waffles blinked.

“You didn’t even look.”

“I don’t need to.”

“But it could be—”

“No.”

“A Halloween tunnel.”

“No.”

“A treasure tunnel?”

“No.”

31 / 33

Waffles smiled.

“What if I have tunnel insurance?”

Pip picked up her briefcase.

“You don’t.”

Waffles hopped toward the hole.

Pip flew after him.

“WAFFLES!”

32 / 33

And off they went—

one white rabbit with questionable coverage,

one tiny insurance salesman,

one woodland army in Halloween costumes,

and one mysterious hole that was absolutely not included in the policy.
