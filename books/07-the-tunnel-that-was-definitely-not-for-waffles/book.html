BOOK 7 · PIP & WAFFLES

Waffles and Pip and the Tunnel That Was Definitely Not for Waffles

An illustrated woodland adventure.

Waffles the white rabbit was minding his own business.

At least that is what Waffles said.

Pip did not believe him.

“Waffles.”

“Yes?”

“Why are you staring at Mole’s tunnel?”

“I am not.”

“You have been staring at it for five minutes.”

“I am looking near it.”

Pip landed beside him.

Waffles leaned closer to the tunnel.

Pip said,

“No.”

Waffles froze.

“I did not say anything.”

“You were about to.”

“I was not.”

“You were thinking about going inside.”

Waffles looked offended.

“I would never.”

1 / 24

From deep inside the tunnel came—

“HELLO!”

Waffles’ ears shot straight up.

Mole poked his head out.

“Come see what I found!”

Waffles looked at Pip.

Pip looked at Waffles.

Waffles whispered,

“He invited me.”

Pip sighed.

“Yes.”

“So now it’s allowed.”

“I suppose.”

2 / 24

Waffles hopped toward the tunnel.

Pip fluttered after him.

“Slowly.”

“I am always slow and careful.”

Waffles immediately tripped over a root.

PLOP.

Pip stared.

Waffles stood back up.

“The root attacked me.”

3 / 24

They followed Mole underground.

The tunnel was dark.

Waffles whispered,

“This is exciting.”

Pip whispered back,

“This is dirt.”

“That is what makes it exciting.”

The tunnel twisted left.

Then right.

Then down.

Then left again.

4 / 24

Waffles stopped.

“Do you know where we are?”

Mole nodded.

“Yes.”

Pip nodded.

“Good.”

Waffles nodded too.

“I also know.”

Pip looked at him.

“No, you don’t.”

“No.”

They kept going.

5 / 24

Then they reached a fork in the tunnel.

One path went left.

One went right.

Mole pointed left.

“We go this way.”

Waffles looked right.

“What’s that way?”

Mole said,

“Nothing.”

Waffles’ eyes widened.

“Nothing?”

“Yes.”

“That sounds suspicious.”

Pip sighed.

“It sounds like nothing.”

6 / 24

Waffles stared at the right tunnel.

“I should investigate.”

“No.”

“Just one peek.”

“No.”

“Half a peek?”

“No.”

Waffles took one tiny step toward it.

Pip said,

“Waffles.”

He stopped.

“Yes?”

“Left.”

Waffles sighed.

“Left.”

7 / 24

They continued.

Soon they heard a strange sound.

Tap.

Tap.

Tap.

Waffles froze.

“What was that?”

Mole listened.

Tap.

Tap.

Tap.

8 / 24

Pip said,

“Probably water.”

Waffles whispered,

“Or a tunnel monster.”

“There are no tunnel monsters.”

“How do you know?”

“Because you just invented one.”

Tap.

Tap.

Tap.

Waffles moved closer to Pip.

“I am not scared.”

“You are standing on my foot.”

“I am being brave nearby.”

9 / 24

They rounded the corner.

Drip.

Drip.

Drip.

Water fell from a root into a tiny puddle.

Waffles stared.

“That’s it?”

Pip nodded.

“That’s it.”

Waffles puffed out his chest.

“I knew that.”

Mole laughed.

10 / 24

They continued until the tunnel opened into a small underground room.

Waffles gasped.

Inside were roots.

Smooth stones.

Tiny mushrooms.

And one old acorn.

Waffles stared at it.

“Treasure.”

Pip looked around.

“It is an acorn.”

“Underground treasure.”

11 / 24

Mole smiled.

“This is my new room.”

Waffles spun around.

“You made this?”

“Yes.”

“It is amazing.”

Mole beamed.

Waffles picked up a smooth stone.

“This can be a chair.”

Pip landed on a root.

“This can be a shelf.”

Mole nodded.

“That’s what I thought!”

12 / 24

Then Waffles noticed another tiny opening in the wall.

His ears rose.

Pip saw his face.

“No.”

Waffles pointed.

“But that tunnel—”

“No.”

“We don’t know where it goes.”

“Exactly.”

“That is the best kind!”

“No.”

13 / 24

Waffles looked at Mole.

Mole shrugged.

“I don’t know where it goes either.”

Waffles gasped.

“UNKNOWN TUNNEL.”

Pip closed her eyes.

“Oh no.”

14 / 24

Waffles hopped toward it.

Pip flew in front of him.

“No.”

“Why?”

“Because we are not crawling into random tunnels.”

Waffles thought.

“But we crawled into this one.”

“Mole knew this one.”

15 / 24

Waffles pointed to the new tunnel.

“Maybe someone knows that one.”

“Who?”

Waffles paused.

“A tunnel person.”

Pip stared.

“A what?”

“I don’t know yet.”

16 / 24

Suddenly—

SCRATCH.

SCRATCH.

SCRATCH.

Something moved inside the mystery tunnel.

Waffles jumped backward.

Pip jumped backward.

Mole jumped backward.

A tiny nose appeared.

Then whiskers.

Then two little eyes.

Mouse popped out.

“Hi.”

17 / 24

Waffles stared.

“YOU.”

Mouse blinked.

“Me?”

“You are the tunnel person.”

Mouse looked confused.

“I was looking for seeds.”

18 / 24

Pip started laughing.

Mole started laughing.

Mouse started laughing.

Waffles did not.

“This tunnel was suspicious.”

Mouse held up a seed.

“I found this.”

Waffles gasped.

“TREASURE.”

Pip laughed harder.

19 / 24

Mouse crawled into Mole’s new room.

“I like this place.”

Mole smiled.

“Want to help decorate?”

Mouse nodded.

Waffles looked around.

Then he pointed at the mystery tunnel.

“So… now that we know where it goes—”

Pip said,

“No.”

Waffles frowned.

“You didn’t even let me finish.”

“I know you.”

Waffles sighed.

20 / 24

Then Mouse whispered,

“There’s another tunnel past mine.”

Waffles’ ears shot straight up.

Pip slowly turned toward Mouse.

“Do not encourage him.”

Mouse smiled.

“Sorry.”

Waffles whispered,

“Another tunnel?”

21 / 24

Pip pointed toward the way they came.

“Home.”

Waffles looked back once.

Then twice.

Then three times.

“Fine.”

22 / 24

They headed toward the surface.

Waffles hopped behind Pip.

After a moment he whispered,

“Pip?”

“Yes?”

“Tomorrow?”

“No.”

“Next week?”

“No.”

“After lunch?”

“No.”

Waffles smiled.

“That was not a never.”

Pip groaned.

And out they went—

one very curious white rabbit,

one exhausted blue bird,

one proud mole,

one seed-hunting mouse,

and at least one tunnel that Waffles was absolutely going to think about all night.
