BOOK 6 · PIP & WAFFLES

Waffles and Pip and the Very Important Picnic

An illustrated woodland adventure.

Waffles the white rabbit had made a decision.

A big decision.

A delicious decision.

He was having a picnic.

Pip the little blue bird landed beside him.

“A picnic?”

“Yes.”

“Who is invited?”

Waffles looked around.

“Everyone.”

Pip blinked.

“Everyone?”

“Everyone I know.”

“That is a lot of everyone.”

Waffles nodded proudly.

“It will be very important.”

“What makes it important?”

Waffles thought.

“The snacks.”

Pip sighed.

“Of course.”

1 / 17

So Waffles and Pip began preparing.

Waffles gathered clover.

Pip found berries.

Mouse brought tiny seeds.

Squirrel brought nuts.

Mole brought…

dirt.

Waffles stared at the pile.

“Mole.”

“Yes?”

“Why did you bring dirt?”

Mole smiled.

“It’s very good dirt.”

Pip whispered, “Just say thank you.”

Waffles nodded.

“Thank you for the dirt.”

Mole beamed.

2 / 17

Then came Hazel the hedgehog.

She carried apples balanced carefully on her back.

Waffles gasped.

“You have a built-in picnic basket!”

Hazel stared at him.

“They are spines.”

“Very useful spines.”

3 / 17

Next came Tilly the turtle.

Very slowly.

Very.

Slowly.

Pip looked down the path.

“Is she coming?”

Waffles squinted.

“I think she moved.”

Three minutes later, Tilly arrived.

“I brought lettuce,” she said.

Waffles looked at Pip.

Pip looked at Waffles.

Waffles whispered, “Detective lettuce?”

“No.”

“Picnic lettuce?”

“Yes.”

“Excellent.”

4 / 17

Then Buzz the bee zipped overhead.

“I brought honey!”

Waffles’ ears popped up.

“HONEY?”

Buzz nodded.

“One drop.”

Waffles stared.

“One?”

“I am a bee, Waffles. Not a grocery store.”

“Fair.”

5 / 17

They spread everything out beneath a big tree.

Waffles stepped back proudly.

“Perfect.”

Pip looked at the food.

“Almost.”

Waffles gasped.

“What is missing?”

“Napkins.”

Waffles looked horrified.

“This picnic is ruined.”

“It is not ruined.”

“No napkins, Pip.”

“We can use leaves.”

Waffles looked at the tree.

“Emergency solved.”

6 / 17

Everyone sat down.

Waffles reached for a strawberry.

Pip slapped his paw gently with one wing.

“Wait.”

“For what?”

“For everyone.”

Waffles looked around.

Mouse was seated.

Squirrel was seated.

Mole was seated.

Hazel was seated.

Buzz hovered.

Tilly was still turning around.

7 / 17

Waffles watched.

And watched.

And watched.

“Is she almost ready?”

Tilly said, “Almost.”

Waffles whispered, “This is difficult.”

Pip whispered back, “You will survive.”

Finally Tilly sat down.

Waffles grabbed the strawberry.

“PICNIC!”

Everyone laughed.

8 / 17

They ate berries.

They ate apples.

They ate clover.

Squirrel ate nuts.

Mouse ate seeds.

Mole ate…

Waffles stopped.

“Mole.”

“Yes?”

“Are you eating the dirt?”

“No.”

“Good.”

“I’m saving it for later.”

Waffles stared.

Pip whispered, “Don’t ask.”

9 / 17

Then suddenly—

WHOOSH!

A gust of wind rushed through the garden.

The leaf napkins flew into the air.

The lettuce flew.

The clover flew.

One apple rolled away.

Waffles jumped up.

“THE PICNIC IS ESCAPING!”

10 / 17

Pip flew after the napkins.

Squirrel chased the apple.

Mouse dove after the seeds.

Hazel caught two leaves on her spines.

Buzz zipped after the clover.

Tilly looked around.

Then very slowly said,

“I will guard the honey.”

Waffles ran in circles.

“Save the strawberries!”

11 / 17

A strawberry rolled down the hill.

Waffles chased it.

“COME BACK!”

It rolled faster.

Waffles hopped faster.

The strawberry bounced.

Waffles bounced.

The strawberry rolled through the grass.

Waffles pounced—

SPLAT.

12 / 17

He landed belly-first in the mud.

The strawberry stopped right beside his nose.

Waffles lifted his head.

“I got it.”

Pip landed nearby.

“You are covered in mud.”

Waffles picked up the strawberry.

“Worth it.”

13 / 17

Back at the picnic blanket, everyone had recovered the food.

Mostly.

Hazel had three leaves stuck to her back.

Mouse had one seed on her head.

Buzz was tangled in clover.

Squirrel was hugging the runaway apple.

And Tilly—

was still guarding the honey.

Waffles smiled.

“We saved the picnic.”

Pip nodded.

“We did.”

14 / 17

Waffles looked at the food.

Then at his friends.

Then at himself.

“I think this was a success.”

Pip laughed.

“You fell in the mud.”

“Successful mud.”

“You chased a strawberry down a hill.”

“Successful strawberry.”

“You screamed that the picnic was escaping.”

Waffles thought.

“That part was also correct.”

Everyone laughed.

15 / 17

Then Waffles noticed one thing.

“Mole.”

“Yes?”

“Where is the dirt?”

Mole pointed to a small empty bowl.

“I ate it.”

Waffles stared.

Pip stared.

Everyone stared.

Mole smiled.

“Very good dirt.”

Waffles nodded slowly.

“I respect your commitment.”

16 / 17

And the picnic continued—

one muddy white rabbit,

one laughing blue bird,

one very patient turtle,

one practical hedgehog,

one busy bee,

one squirrel,

one mouse,

one mole,

and absolutely no surviving napkins.

The End
