PIP & WAFFLES

Waffles and Pip Investigate the Garden

An illustrated woodland adventure.

Waffles the white rabbit stood at the edge of the garden.

He wore a leaf on his head.

Pip the little blue bird landed beside him.

“Why are you wearing that?”

“It is my detective hat.”

“It is lettuce.”

“Detective lettuce.”

Pip sighed.

Waffles pointed toward the garden.

“Something suspicious is happening.”

1 / 21

Pip looked around.

The flowers were blooming.

The bees were buzzing.

The sun was shining.

A ladybug walked across a leaf.

“What is suspicious?”

Waffles whispered,

“Everything.”

Pip blinked.

“That seems like a lot.”

Waffles hopped into the garden.

“Investigation begins NOW.”

2 / 21

First, they examined the tomatoes.

Waffles stared at one.

The tomato stared back.

At least Waffles thought it did.

“Very suspicious.”

“It’s a tomato.”

“It is red.”

“Yes.”

“Why?”

“Because it is ripe.”

Waffles narrowed his eyes.

“That is exactly what a suspicious tomato would say.”

“The tomato didn’t say anything.”

“Even more suspicious.”

Pip flew to the next garden row.

“Come look at this.”

Waffles hurried over.

There were tiny holes in the lettuce leaves.

Waffles gasped.

“THE CRIMINAL HAS STRUCK.”

Pip leaned closer.

“Looks like something has been nibbling.”

Waffles looked horrified.

“NIBBLING?”

He turned slowly.

“Who would do such a thing?”

Pip stared at him.

Waffles stared back.

Pip looked at his mouth.

A little piece of lettuce was stuck to his lip.

Waffles quickly swallowed it.

“That proves nothing.”

They searched for clues.

Pip found a tiny green caterpillar.

Waffles crouched beside it.

“State your name.”

The caterpillar kept eating.

“Where were you last night?”

The caterpillar kept eating.

“Do you have an alibi?”

Crunch.

Crunch.

Crunch.

Pip laughed.

“I think you found your lettuce nibbler.”

Waffles gasped.

“The suspect is still eating the evidence!”

The caterpillar crawled onto another leaf.

Waffles whispered,

“Bold.”

Pip gently moved the caterpillar to a patch of wild plants outside the garden.

“Problem solved.”

Waffles puffed out his chest.

“Excellent detective work.”

“You didn’t do anything.”

“I supervised.”

They continued.

Near the carrots, they found a small mound of dirt.

Waffles froze.

“Pip.”

“Yes?”

“There has been digging.”

Pip looked at him.

“You dig all the time.”

“Exactly.”

Waffles looked around nervously.

“There may be another rabbit.”

Pip tilted her head.

“You are worried about another rabbit?”

“No.”

Waffles paused.

“Yes.”

A tiny nose popped out of the dirt.

Waffles jumped backward.

“AAAH!”

A mole climbed out.

The mole blinked.

Waffles blinked.

Pip blinked.

The mole said,

“Hello.”

Waffles whispered,

“He talks.”

“So do you,” said Pip.

“Different.”

The mole dusted off his paws.

“I’m just making a tunnel.”

Waffles’ ears perked up.

“A tunnel?”

“Yes.”

“Where does it go?”

The mole shrugged.

“Down.”

Waffles smiled.

Pip immediately said,

“No.”

“I didn’t say anything.”

“You were thinking about going in.”

“I was not.”

“You absolutely were.”

Waffles looked into the hole.

“Maybe just one little—”

“No.”

They moved on.

Waffles stopped.

His nose twitched.

His eyes widened.

Pip landed in front of him.

“No.”

“I haven’t done anything.”

“You are thinking loudly.”

Waffles stared at the berries.

“One strawberry.”

“No.”

“Half a strawberry.”

“No.”

“A very small investigation bite?”

“Waffles.”

“That is not a thing.”

Pip fluttered quietly.

RUSTLE.

Closer.

Closer.

A squirrel sat beside a pile of sunflower seeds.

Waffles froze.

Crunch.

“Where did you put it?”

“The mole dug the dirt.”
